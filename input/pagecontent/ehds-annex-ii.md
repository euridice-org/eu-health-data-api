# EHDS Regulation (EU) 2025/327 — Annex II

> **Canonical source:** [EUR-Lex — EHDS Annex II](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/327/oj/eng#anx_II)
>
> Published: 5 March 2025 | Entered into force: 26 March 2025

This IG references the essential requirements for EHR systems laid down in EHDS Annex II. The table below maps Annex II sections to their interpretation in this IG. For the authoritative text, refer to EUR-Lex.

| Annex II Section | Title | Relevance to this IG |
|---|---|---|
| §1.4 | Compatibility with Other Products | Grounds the authorization and transport security requirements ([Authorization](authorization.html)) |
| §2.1 | Data Access Interface | Grounds the Access Provider actor and document/resource query APIs ([Actors](actors.html), [Document Exchange](document-exchange.html), [Resource Access](resource-access.html)) |
| §2.2 | Data Reception Capability | Grounds the Consumer actor and ability to receive EEHRxF data ([Actors](actors.html)) |
| §2.4 | Data Entry Granularity | Grounds the content profile requirements (handled by HL7 EU Content IGs) |
| §3.1 | Professional Authentication | Informs the authorization scope discussion ([Authorization](authorization.html)) |
